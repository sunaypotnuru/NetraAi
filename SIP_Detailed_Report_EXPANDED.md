## ABOUT THE ORGANIZATION

### National Thermal Power Corporation Limited (NTPC) - A Comprehensive Overview

National Thermal Power Corporation Limited, universally known by its acronym NTPC, represents India's largest energy conglomerate and stands among the world's most prominent power generation companies. The organization's establishment dates back to 1975, a watershed moment in India's post-independence industrial development when the Government of India recognized the critical need for a dedicated organization capable of building and operating large-scale power plants to meet the nation's rapidly growing electricity demands.
